Engine Acoustic Diagnostics
Cars · Bikes · Marine · Any Combustion Engine
What it is

Every engine has a unique acoustic signature — the sum of every firing event, every valve, every bearing, every spinning shaft. An AcouCheck reads that signature and turns it into a clear, written report. No expensive workshop bills. No guesswork. Just an honest read on how your engine is actually running.

What We Find

A full health check from sound alone.

Our acoustic analysis spots issues invisible to a basic OBD scan. Each report covers the following checks with plain-English findings and clear recommendations.

01

Cylinder Balance

Is each cylinder pulling its weight? Weak cylinders show up immediately.

02

Misfire Signature

Detects irregular firing patterns before they trigger a fault code.

03

Knock & Detonation

Pre-ignition events that quietly destroy power and pistons.

04

Turbo Health

Bearing condition, surge events, shaft resonance — caught early.

05

Valve Train

Tappet rattle, valve lash, timing chain stretch — all have a sound.

06

Bearing Wear

Sub-harmonic resonances reveal bearings approaching end of life.

07

Injector Consistency

A clean injector sounds different to a tired one. We hear both.

08

Calibration Sanity

Tachometer accuracy, idle stability, throttle response, sensor offsets.

How It Works

Three steps. One honest report.

01
We Record

A short recording captured at the engine while it idles and revs. No wires into the ECU. No disassembly.

02
We Analyse

The acoustic signature is decomposed against your specific engine configuration — cylinder count, fuel type, forced induction.

03
You Get The Report

A clean, written diagnostic. What's healthy. What needs watching. What needs attention now. Plain English.
