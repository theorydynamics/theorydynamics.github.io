FUSESCAN

Weld Inspection Software

Photograph. Scan. Report.

Take a photograph of any weld with your phone or PC camera. FUSESCAN analyses the image and delivers a full inspection report in seconds — what the defect is, where it is, and what to do about it.

Plain English Reports

No specialist training required. Every defect comes with a clear description, severity rating, and recommended corrective action. Your welders understand the results immediately.

Catch It Before The Inspector Does

FUSESCAN is a pre-screening tool that helps welders identify problems before formal inspection. Fix defects early, reduce rework, and pass first time.

No Special Equipment

Runs on any Windows PC. Works with any camera. No special lighting, no calibration, no laboratory. Software on a laptop — that's all you need.

Defect Detection

Cracks, porosity, undercut, lack of fusion, spatter, burn through, misalignment, slag inclusions, overlap, surface roughness, and more. With confidence scores and severity ratings on every finding.

Flexible Licensing

From single welders to large fabrication companies. Free trial available. Monthly and yearly options to suit your operation. Contact us for a demo.
