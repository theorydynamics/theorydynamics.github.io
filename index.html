Our Approach
Why This Works

The hardware in modern engines, turbines, and industrial systems has improved enormously over forty years. The software controlling that hardware has not. We close that gap.

⚡
Live Physics, Not Static Tables

Conventional control systems read pre-calculated values from a grid. Our systems calculate the optimal output from first-principles physics in real time — every cycle, adapting to actual conditions as they change.

⟳
Self-Learning Adaptation

Our algorithms learn the characteristics of each individual system within the first operating cycles. Different fuel, different wear, different conditions — the physics adapts automatically without manual tuning.

◈
Same Hardware, Better Results

We don't require new sensors, new actuators, or new equipment. The existing hardware in most modern systems is already capable of far more than its current software demands. We unlock that potential.

△
Clean and Profitable

Better combustion means less fuel burned for the same output. That reduces costs and reduces emissions in the same upgrade. You don't have to choose between performance and environmental responsibility.

▣
Multi-Domain Expertise

Our physics framework applies across scales and industries — from motorcycle engines to power station boilers. The principles are universal. The implementation is tailored to each application.

About
Theory Dynamics

Theory Dynamics was founded by Craig Gillatt — an engineer and inventor based in Tuxford, Nottinghamshire. The company holds UK Patent #2522970.9 and develops proprietary physics-based control systems across multiple industries.

Our approach is built on a simple observation: the hardware in modern engines and industrial systems has improved enormously over the past four decades. Sensors are more precise. Materials are more capable. Processing power has increased a thousandfold. But the fundamental software philosophy controlling these systems has remained largely unchanged since the mid-1980s.

We close that gap. Our systems replace static lookup tables with live physics calculations — using the full processing power of modern hardware to optimise performance in real time. The result is better output, lower fuel consumption, and reduced emissions from existing equipment.

Theory Dynamics works with clients ranging from individual vehicle owners to industrial operators and aerospace companies. Whether you need a single engine flash or a plant-wide optimisation programme, the physics scales.
